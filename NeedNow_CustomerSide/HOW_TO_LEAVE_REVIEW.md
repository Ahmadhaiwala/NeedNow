# How Buyers Leave Reviews - User Guide

## 🎯 Complete Buyer Review Process

### Overview
After a seller accepts your offer, you can leave a review to rate your experience. This helps build trust in the marketplace community.

---

## 📋 Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Make an Offer
1. Browse the marketplace
2. Find a post/listing you're interested in
3. Click on the post to view details
4. Click "Make Offer" button
5. Enter your offer price and optional message
6. Submit the offer

### Step 2: Wait for Seller to Accept
- The seller will review your offer
- You'll see the offer status in "My Offers"
- When accepted, status changes to "✅ Accepted"

### Step 3: Access "My Offers" Section

**Location:** Marketplace page header

Look for this button:
```
┌──────────────────────────┐
│ 💵 My Offers            │  ← Click here
└──────────────────────────┘
```

The button is located at the top of the marketplace page, next to:
- "Messages" button
- "Post Need or Sell Item" button

### Step 4: Find Your Accepted Offer

The "My Offers" modal opens showing:
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Offers Management                       │
│                                         │
│ [All] [Pending] [Accepted] [Rejected]  │ ← Filter tabs
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                         │
│ ✅ Accepted                             │
│ ₹500                                    │
│ Listing: Gaming Laptop                  │
│ "Great price!"                          │
│                                         │
│                    [⭐ Leave Review]    │ ← HERE!
│                                         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Other offers...                         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step 5: Click "Leave Review"

When you click the "⭐ Leave Review" button:
- A review modal pops up
- Seller's name is pre-filled
- Post title is shown

### Step 6: Submit Your Review

The review form includes:

**1. Star Rating (Required):**
```
☆☆☆☆☆  Click stars to rate 1-5
```
- 1 star = Poor
- 2 stars = Below Average  
- 3 stars = Average
- 4 stars = Good
- 5 stars = Excellent

**2. Comment (Optional):**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Share details about punctuality,   │
│ item condition, or communication... │
│                                     │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**3. Submit:**
```
[Cancel]  [Submit Rating & Review]
```

### Step 7: Review Submitted! ✅

After submission:
- Review appears on **seller's profile**
- Seller's rating updates immediately
- You see a success message
- Modal closes

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: When can I leave a review?
**A:** Only after the seller accepts your offer. The "Leave Review" button appears on accepted offers in "My Offers".

### Q: Can I review before the seller accepts?
**A:** No. Reviews are only available for accepted offers.

### Q: Can I edit my review later?
**A:** Yes! If you open the review modal again for the same post, it will show your existing review and let you edit it.

### Q: Where does my review appear?
**A:** Your review appears on the **seller's profile page**, showing their reputation to other buyers.

### Q: Does my review show on my profile?
**A:** No. Reviews you **write** about sellers don't appear on your profile. Only reviews others write **about you** appear on your profile.

### Q: Can the seller review me back?
**A:** No. Only buyers can review sellers in marketplace transactions. This prevents review manipulation.

### Q: What if I can't find the "My Offers" button?
**A:** Make sure you're:
- Logged in
- On the marketplace page (`/marketplace`)
- Looking at the top header area near "Messages"

### Q: What if there's no "Leave Review" button?
**A:** The button only appears if:
- ✅ Your offer was accepted
- ✅ You haven't withdrawn the offer
- Filter is set to show "Accepted" offers

## 🔍 Troubleshooting

### Problem: "My Offers" button not visible
**Solution:** 
- Ensure you're logged in
- Navigate to the Marketplace page
- Button is in the header next to Messages

### Problem: No "Leave Review" button on accepted offer
**Solution:**
- Make sure offer status is "Accepted" 
- Click the "Accepted" filter tab to show only accepted offers
- Refresh the My Offers modal

### Problem: Review submission fails
**Possible reasons:**
- You're trying to review yourself (not possible)
- Offer isn't actually accepted
- You're not the buyer (can't review if you're the seller)

**Solution:** Check error message for details
